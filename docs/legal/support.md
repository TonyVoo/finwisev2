## Common questions

**My data is not on my new phone.** Sign in with the same email and password; your records download on the
first sync. You need to be online once.

**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen choose _Forgot password?_. We email a one-time code. No email?
Check spam, and that you used the address you signed up with.

**How do I delete my account and my data?** Settings → Delete account. It needs you to be online, and permanently
deletes your account and records. Export first (Settings → Export expenses) if you want a copy. If you own a household
with other members, transfer or delete it first.

**Can I use it offline?** Yes. Adding, editing and viewing work offline, and changes sync when you are back
online.

**Why was my receipt read wrongly?** Reading is a suggestion from an outside service and can be wrong; check the
amount before pressing Use. Flat, well-lit photos read best.

## Reporting a problem

Please include the app version (shown in your phone's app settings or the store page), your phone model and system version, what you did, and what
you expected. Please **do not send your password**.
