**Full description:**

```text
Know where your money goes, with or without a signal.

Expense Tracker keeps everything on your phone first, so adding an expense is instant and never waits on the network. When you are online it syncs quietly, and your records are there on a new phone too.

ADD IT FAST
- A big keypad, a category, a date, done.
- Type a sentence like "coffee 45k yesterday" and tap Fill: the app reads it on your phone and offers the amount, date and category for you to confirm.
- Photograph a receipt and keep it with the expense. Optionally, let the app read the total, date and shop for you to check.

SEE THE MONTH
- A monthly summary with a category chart and a six-month trend.
- Plain-language insights: spending against last month, your biggest category, and how much of a budget is used.
- Set a monthly budget for any category and get a gentle warning before you pass it.
- Optional: ask for a short written recap of the month.

STAY ON TOP OF BILLS AND GOALS
- Recurring expenses with reminders before they are due.
- Savings goals you can track toward.

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
- Invite people with a code and keep one set of books together.
- Mark an expense private and it stays hidden from the others.
- Split shared costs equally, see who owes whom, and record when someone pays up.

BUILT TO BE PRIVATE
- Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode.
- Export everything as a CSV whenever you like, and delete your account and data inside the app.
- No ads, no tracking, no bank logins.

EVERYDAY DETAILS
- Pay in another currency and the app converts it with the day's rate.
- Light and dark themes, English and Vietnamese.

Some features are optional and send a photo, a sentence or the month's summary sentences to an outside service (Google Gemini) only when you tap them; the app says so beside each button, and on a free plan that service may use what it receives to improve its products. Everything else stays between your phone and our server.
```

## Apple

**Age rating questionnaire.** Answer _None_ / _No_ to every content question (violence, sexual content, profanity,
gambling, horror, drugs, medical advice and the rest). _Unrestricted web access_: No. The app has no chat and no
public content: household members see each other's shared expenses, nothing more. Expected result: **4+**; read the
rating the form gives you rather than assuming it.

**Export compliance.** Already declared in the app (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption: false`): it uses only the
standard HTTPS encryption of the operating system. Confirm that is how you want to answer.

**Sign in with Apple.** Not required: the app offers email and password only. It becomes required the moment any other
third-party login is added.

**App Review notes** (paste, then fill the account):

> Sign in is by email and password. Demo account: `[EMAIL]` / `[PASSWORD]` (it has sample expenses in two months, one
> category with a budget, and a household with one other member). The app is offline-first: it works without a
> connection and syncs when online. Three optional features send data to Google's Gemini API only when tapped, and
> say so beside the button: _Read receipt_, quick add's _Fill_ on a sentence it cannot read itself, and _Write my
> recap_ on the Summary screen. They need a key on our server; if you do not see the buttons, the key is off.
> Account deletion is in Settings. Face ID is used only for the optional app lock.

## Google Play

**Data safety** - the same facts in Play's terms:

| Play category       | Data                                             | Collected | Shared    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------- | --------- |
| Personal info       | Email address; name (optional)                   | Yes       | No        |
| Financial info      | Other financial info (expenses, income, budgets) | Yes       | No        |
| Photos and videos   | Photos (receipt photos you attach)               | Yes       | See below |
| App activity        | Other user-generated content (notes)             | Yes       | No        |
| Device or other IDs | User ID                                          | Yes       | No        |

- **Encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS). **You can request deletion:** Yes, inside the app (Settings, Delete account).
- **Sharing:** the three optional features send a photo, a sentence or summary sentences to Google's Gemini API when the
  person taps them. Play treats a service provider that processes data on your behalf as _not sharing_, but a
  free-tier provider that may use it to improve its own products is the case to read Play's definition for and, if in
  doubt, declare as sharing.
- **Account deletion URL.** Play requires a web page (not only the in-app control) where a person can ask to delete
  their account and data: `[ACCOUNT DELETION URL]`. `docs/legal/support.md` already explains the in-app steps; host it
  and use its address here, or add a short section for people who no longer have the app.
- **Content rating (IARC):** answer _No_ to violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances and gambling;
  it is a finance utility with no user-to-user communication beyond a household's shared expenses.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over is the simplest honest choice for a finance app; the privacy policy says it is not
  directed at children under `[13 / 16]`.
